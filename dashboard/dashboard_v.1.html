Dynamic Price Model P(t): P(t) = Dynamic Market Cap(t) / AXON Circulating Supply(t)
Dynamic Market Cap: Launch (0-60d: 6.76M→443M), Growth (60d-1y: 443M→5B), Mature (1-3y: 5B×1.5^years), Stable (3y+: 10% annual growth). Dynamic Reward Model N(t): N(t) = Block Reward(t) × Blocks per Month × Network Share
Block reward based on AXON whitepaper formula(31): v(x) = λ + (1-λ)·a^(-x/(N-x)) where λ=0.01, a=2.5, 3秒区块时间, 矿机数量变化: 50K→300K台(24个月S型增长)
Monthly Revenue Model R(t): R(t) = ∑[Monthly Reward(t) × Token Amount(t) × Token Price(t)]
Discrete monthly revenue calculation: R(t) = ∑[N(t) × P(t)] where N(t) is monthly token reward, P(t) is token price
Revenue Model R(t): R(t) = ∫[N(t) × P(t)]dt
Cumulative revenue from dynamic mining rewards times dynamic token price
Payback Period: Find t where R(t) ≥ C(t)
